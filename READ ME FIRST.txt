APIS - your personal operations hub
===================================
Everything stays on your laptop. It costs nothing.

STEP 1: Install Python (one time only, free)
  1. Go to https://www.python.org/downloads/ and download Python.
  2. Run the installer. On the FIRST screen, tick "Add python.exe to PATH". Then press Install Now.

STEP 2: Start APIS
  Double-click "Start APIS". A black window opens (leave it open) and your browser opens APIS.
  The first time, choose a PIN. You will type it each time you open APIS.
  If Windows asks about the firewall, press "Allow access" (this lets your phone connect).

STEP 3 (optional): Start APIS by itself
  Double-click "Start APIS when Windows starts". After that APIS is always ready at
  http://localhost:8501. To undo it, double-click "Stop starting APIS with Windows".

USING APIS ON YOUR ANDROID PHONE AND TABLET
  1. Laptop on, APIS running, phone on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. In APIS on the laptop open Settings and Data. Under "Use APIS on your phone and tablet"
     you will see an address like http://192.168.0.12:8501
  3. Type that address into Chrome on the phone or tablet and sign in with your PIN.
  4. Chrome menu (three dots), then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen". APIS now has its own icon.

YOUR DATA
  Saved in the "data" folder inside this folder:
    apis.db       all your records
    uploads       your photos, logos and PDFs
    backups       automatic copies, made every few hours
  GOOGLE DRIVE BACKUP: install "Google Drive for desktop", then in APIS open Settings and Data,
  paste a folder inside your Google Drive into "Google Drive backup folder", and press
  "Save and back up now".
  RESTORE ON A NEW LAPTOP: install Python, unzip APIS, close APIS, then copy the saved
  "database" and "uploads" folders from your Drive backup into APIS\data\ and start APIS.
  (The backup also holds apis_data.xlsx, a readable Excel copy of everything.)

EMAIL REPORTS
  In APIS open Settings and Data, then "Gmail for reports", and follow the 5 short steps for an App Password.
  Then open Reports and Automation, switch on the automatic daily email and choose the time.
  The laptop must be on and APIS running at that time.

FORGOT YOUR PIN?
  Close APIS, then double-click "Reset my PIN" and type a new one. Your records are not touched.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  - "Python is not installed": do Step 1 again and remember the PATH tick.
  - The page does not open: open your browser and type http://localhost:8501
  - Phone cannot connect: same Wi-Fi? Laptop awake? Firewall allowed?
  - Details of any error are in data\apis.log

PRIVACY
  Your PIN protects APIS on your Wi-Fi. Do not store customer ID numbers, account numbers or
  passwords in APIS. Check every figure against official reports before using it at work.
